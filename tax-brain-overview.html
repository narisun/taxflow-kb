What is Tax Brain?

Tax Brain is a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) knowledge base built specifically for CPAs. It ingests IRS publications, structures them into a navigable topic ontology, and provides an AI query agent that answers tax questions the way a CPA thinks — starting from general guidance, then drilling into the specific rules, thresholds, and forms that apply.

Unlike generic document search, Tax Brain understands the relationships between tax concepts. A question about rental property depreciation doesn't just search Pub 527 — it knows to also pull MACRS rules from Pub 946 and basis calculations from Pub 551, because that's what a CPA would do.

Architecture: How Tax Brain Answers Questions

The system uses a five-layer pipeline with a hierarchical retrieval strategy that mirrors how CPAs navigate tax guidance — from general scope to specific detail.

Natural language question with intent classification (LOOKUP, SCENARIO, FORM_LINE, RULE, COMPARISON, GENERAL)
↓

Three-Tier Chunk Hierarchy

Every IRS publication is indexed at three levels of granularity. This prevents the "lost in information" problem — the system always knows where it is in the knowledge base and can navigate up (for context) or down (for specifics).

Tier 1   Publication Summaries

One anchor per publication. Contains: table of contents, key forms referenced, dollar thresholds, and topic ontology mapping. Answers "What does this pub cover?"

Tier 2   Section Summaries

One anchor per major chapter/section. Contains: section scope, first paragraph overview, sub-section list, key thresholds and forms. Answers "What rules does this section contain?"

Tier 3   Detail Chunks

~400-token passages from the actual PDF text, with 50-token overlap. Contains: chapter/section context, form/line cross-references, embedded dollar amounts. Answers the specific question.

Example: "What is the MACRS recovery period for residential rental property?"
Tier 1 identifies Pub 946 (Depreciation) and Pub 527 (Rental Property) as relevant.
Tier 2 narrows to "Chapter: MACRS" in Pub 946 and "Chapter: Depreciation" in Pub 527.
Tier 3 retrieves the specific passage: "Residential rental property has a 27.5-year recovery period using straight-line depreciation."

How a Query Flows Through Tax Brain

Example query: "My client sold a rental property they owned for 8 years. How do we handle the depreciation recapture?"
1

Query Classification

Intent: SCENARIO. Detected topics: rental property, depreciation, capital gains. Tax year: current (2025).

2

Stage 1 — Navigate Summaries

Searches Tier 1 and Tier 2 anchor chunks. Identifies: Pub 527 (Rental Property), Pub 946 (Depreciation), Pub 544 (Sales of Assets).

3

Ontology Augment

Topic graph finds "depreciation recapture" links to Pub 551 (Basis of Assets) as a supplementary reference. Adds it to scope.

4

Stage 2 — Drill into Details

Vector + BM25 hybrid search scoped to Pubs 527, 946, 544, 551 only. Retrieves 10 detail chunks covering: Section 1250 recapture rules, 27.5-year recovery period, unrecaptured gain rates, and adjusted basis calculations.

5

LLM Synthesis

GPT-4o composes a structured answer: depreciation recapture at 25% rate, remaining gain at long-term capital gains rates, with citations to specific publication sections and relevant forms (Form 4797, Schedule D).

Query Types Tax Brain Handles

LOOKUP — Specific thresholds and limits

"What is the 2025 IRA contribution limit for someone over 50?"

SCENARIO — Multi-step client fact patterns

"Client sold rental property after 8 years. How is depreciation recaptured?"

FORM_LINE — Form and schedule questions

"What goes on Schedule C, line 31?"

RULE — MeF validation and filing rules

"What triggers the requirement to file Form 8960?"

COMPARISON — Year-over-year or option analysis

"Standard deduction vs itemized for a single filer in 2025?"

GENERAL — Broad guidance questions

"How are health savings accounts taxed?"
